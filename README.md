### Engineering principles I care about

- **Clarity over accidental complexity** — a good abstraction should make change easier.
- **Reliability over happy-path demos** — failures, retries, observability, and recovery matter.
- **Evidence over hype** — experiments and measurements should inform design decisions.
- **Human judgment with AI assistance** — AI can accelerate work without replacing ownership.
